# Claim–Evidence Map V1
## Counterexample-Guided Invariant Skills for ARC-AGI-3

**Purpose:** one-page research map linked to [Project Idea V3](arc_prize_2026_project_idea_v3.md). This is a claim-planning artifact—not a new readiness phase, preregistration, run authorization, or result.  
**Status rule:** “partial” means only the stated synthetic mechanism is supported; it does not transfer to ARC-AGI-3. No AGI-3 runs/results are present in the workspace.

## Claim chain and paper ceiling

`A: candidate generalizes + N: invariant adds value beyond replay → B: counterexamples improve scope → C: scope reduces harmful transfer → D: less harm improves held-out efficiency → E: full method outperforms baselines`

Each arrow needs evidence. If A–C fail, do not proceed to a causal transfer claim. If D fails, report the mechanism result without claiming action-efficiency benefit. If E fails, preserve the null/negative result and narrow the paper claim. Do not jump from a Phase 13 synthetic pass to an ARC-AGI-3 claim or to “intelligence growth.”

## Current project status / constraints

- **Supported narrowly:** synthetic operation patterns and some synthetic skill reuse/cost-proxy effects in the known generator.
- **Not established:** open-ended invariant discovery, counterexample-based scope improvement, AGI-3 transfer, causal reduction in negative transfer, or superiority over replay baselines.
- **Still unauthorized/blocked:** Phase 2I candidate-production run; Phase 3B; Phase 3D. Phase 3A remains implementation plumbing only. This map does not authorize experiments or hidden/test outcome access.
- **Not a new readiness phase:** this page only ties claims to evidence and defines what would count as support or failure.
- **Eligibility:** user reports ARC-AGI-3 team membership and a late-submission route. Whether a late, code-prize-ineligible entry satisfies Paper Prize linkage/Accuracy remains unresolved separately from the research claims.

## Decision

The project has a clear, falsifiable research path. **A has only limited known-generator synthetic support; N and B–E are untested for the proposed ARC-AGI-3 method.** Novelty remains a hypothesis pending focused comparison with skill induction, counterexample-guided synthesis, option applicability learning, causal skill evaluation, world-model verification, and current ARC-AGI-3 systems.
