# Phase 2F.8 — Bottleneck Attribution Framework

Assign a category only after budget/code/trace/downstream parity and denominator checks pass. Report the evidence chain and uncertainty; labels do not automatically establish a root cause.

| Code | Operational classification | Minimum evidence | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1 Representation Dominant (supported)** | Alternative produces a preregistered material coverage increase over R1 and downstream activation progresses reproducibly beyond trace yield into accepted/validated invariants and later eligibility stages. | Paired common-task comparison; fixed search/budgets/downstream/evaluator; HCC passes; independent repeat; lower interval bound above preregistered `δ_coverage` and activation criterion. | Supports a representation effect in this pipeline; does not prove sole cause or theory validation. |
| **B2 Representation Contributing** | Coverage improves materially, but trace diversity/support or invariant/skill activation remains blocked. | Coverage contrast passes prespecified margin; stage cascade remains measurable. | Representation mattered upstream, but did not explain downstream failure. |
| **B3 Representation Weak (for tested alternative)** | The specified alternative does not materially improve coverage under a valid matched comparison. | Implementation/HCC pass, budget parity, sufficient precision and independent replication. | Weakens only the tested alternative; does not prove the current representation is adequate or all alternatives fail. |
| **B4 Mixed Bottlenecks** | Evidence implicates multiple stages (e.g., coverage gain plus low recurrence; search/parameter failure plus parser loss; invariant gain plus skill-adapter block). | Stage-resolved paired metrics and named competing factors. | State each contribution separately; do not force one primary cause. |
| **B5 Unresolved** | Parity, HCC, implementation, labels, power/precision, or endpoint integrity fails; or evidence cannot distinguish classifications. | Any critical gate unavailable or contradictory. | Report unavailable/blocked; do not convert to evidence for/against representation. |

## Causal safeguards

- R0 historical is context only; it is not the matched causal baseline.
- R1 vs R2–R7 holds common search, candidate cap, resources, evaluator, trace contract, support policy, skill/transfer/capability code fixed. Search and representation are not co-tuned after results.
- A budgeted no-fit is not semantic unrepresentability. Parser errors, candidate exhaustion, and support attrition remain separate.
- Coverage is not transfer, capability, or accuracy. A high-coverage control validates measurement plumbing only.
- Any positive mechanism claim states “compared to what?” and “under identical conditions?”; a score/coverage rise alone is not theory validation.
