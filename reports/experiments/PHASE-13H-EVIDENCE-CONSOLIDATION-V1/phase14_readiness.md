# Phase 14 Readiness Review

## Decision

**Proceed with Phase 14 as evidence review, claim restriction, and interpretation of the saved Phase 13 record. Do not treat Phase 13 as confirmatory support for the full theory or as ARC validation.** Phase 13H deliverables are complete; the key scientific gates are not all passed.

## Gate-by-gate readiness

| Area | Status entering Phase 14 | Reason / claim ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| IDT discovery | Ready for bounded analysis | Synthetic recurrence is present, but small candidate populations and generator-known rules constrain generalization. |
| IS→transfer | Not supported | Perfect Phase 13B association is fragile; corrected Phase 13D association is near zero/non-positive; Phase 13G unavailable. |
| Transfer-aware ranking | Gate failed | Original Phase 13B strict baseline rule failed. EXP-IDT-14 is a later exploratory stress test only. |
| SCT | Partial synthetic evidence | Reflection/rotation skill formation/reuse exists, but Phase 13C policy did not beat the required alternatives and SCV's incremental contribution was not established. |
| Transfer/composition | Partial / unavailable | Synthetic transfer and step savings occurred; full structural composition was untestable in 13D due missing component skills. |
| CGM | Mixed and metric-bound | Positive Phase 13E CGR/IG, adverse IE trend and composition value, negative Phase 13D mean CGR, mixed 13F joint baseline results. |
| ARC | Not demonstrated | Phase 13G had very low bounded-DSL fit coverage, no invariants/skills/capabilities, and identical 0.5% exact holdout accuracy across arms. |
| Baselines | Not globally passed | Failures vs original 13B controls, 13C memory-only, corrected 13D B4, 13E B1, and 13F B1/B3. |
| Statistics | Descriptive only across phases | Incompatible task generators and units; few seeds/clusters; near-zero variance; unadjusted secondary contrasts; repeated 13G holdout. |

## Recommended Phase 14 work

1. **Freeze the evidence.** Treat the Phase 13H source manifest and cited saved reports as the analysis record. Do not rerun 13C–13G or rewrite their raw results.
2. **Use the corrected views, not the broken dashboards.** The Phase 13D corrected posthoc analysis is authoritative for saved-record baseline/composition comparisons. Preserve frozen defective reporter artifacts for audit. Phase 13E/F posthoc corrections remain reporting-only.
3. **Keep gates unchanged.** Preserve the failed original Phase 13B `baseline_comparison`, its seed-level strict-superiority rule, and original synthetic-v1 data. EXP-IDT-14 remains exploratory; do not make a retrospective pass.
4. **Report per phase and endpoint.** Include estimate, SD/interval, independent unit, metric definition, comparator, missingness, scope, and status. Do not average or meta-analyze different phases.
5. **Separate software correctness from evidence.** Contract tests, reproducible outputs, trace logging, and dashboard correctness support implementation claims only; they are not benchmark success.
6. **State ARC limits explicitly.** Do not describe Phase 13G as an official ARC score. Preserve the low DSL coverage, public training/evaluation distinction, archive handling disclosure, attempt-01 prediction exposure, and same-holdout rerun.
7. **Use a narrow narrative.** Report synthetic mechanism demonstrations and null/adverse outcomes together. Do not call CGR/IG/IGR “intelligence growth” without a separate construct-validity argument.

## Claim ceiling for downstream writing

Recommended text:

> “Across controlled synthetic ReasonFlux tasks, the implementation can detect recurring operation signatures, form a limited set of reflection/rotation skills, and exhibit measured reuse and capability-metric growth in some frozen settings. However, the original transfer-aware ranking gate failed, SCT and baseline comparisons were mixed or adverse, invariant strength did not reliably predict transfer, capability-growth diagnostics were inconsistent, and the bounded Phase 13G ARC adapter did not instantiate the mechanism chain. These results demonstrate software behavior and selected synthetic effects, not general intelligence or ARC generalization.”

## Explicit no-go claims

- “ASFT beats all baselines.”
- “IS robustly predicts transfer.”
- “SCV is superior to frequency/no-SCV selection.”
- “Composition is a generally validated causal mechanism.”
- “CGR/IG establish general capability or intelligence growth.”
- “ASFT solves ARC” or “Phase 13G is an official ARC-AGI-2 evaluation.”
- Any statement that EXP-IDT-14 repairs Phase 13B or that the Phase 13G same-holdout rerun is independent confirmation.
