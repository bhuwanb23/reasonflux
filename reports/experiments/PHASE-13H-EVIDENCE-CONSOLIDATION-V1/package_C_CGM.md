# Package C — Capability Growth and Metrics (CGM)

**Question:** Do capability portfolios grow across rounds, does composition create value, and do IG/IGR/IE support an intelligence-growth interpretation?

A capability persisted across consecutive rounds in 10/10 seeds (criterion ≥8/10). There were zero accepted higher-order emergent capability occurrences; 0/10 seeds met the descriptive post-composition capability-count-growth condition. Composition-value mean was `-0.1429`, not positive. TS→CGR association was `r=-0.503` (95% CI `[-0.860,0.186]`); SU→CGR was `r=0.993` (CI `[0.969,0.998]`), descriptive n=10. ASFT-minus-B1 all-task utility was `-0.0361` despite a positive final-capability-count contrast. The latter does not erase the utility failure.

## Cross-phase constraints

- Corrected Phase 13D mean CGR was `-0.060` over nine evaluable seeds; TS→CGR `r=0.483` was descriptive, not a causal effect or evidence of positive mean CGR.
- Phase 13F's joint baseline decision required both all-task utility and CGR to improve. It failed versus B1 (utility `-0.03634`, CGR `+0.28717`) and B3 (both `0`); it passed versus B2 and B4. Some removed components changed CGR without changing task utility. Standardized paired effects were extreme where paired SD was near zero and should not be read as huge practical effects.
- Phase 13G had no validated capability groups: CGR/IG/IGR are unavailable, not zero-valued positive or negative findings.

## Judgment

The Phase 13E results show growth of the repository's defined synthetic capability metrics in one cohort. They do not establish robust cross-phase capability growth, causality, broad generalization, or intelligence growth. The IE trend, composition-value, association, cross-phase, baseline, and ARC findings materially limit interpretation.

## Defensible claim

> “The frozen Phase 13E synthetic pipeline produced positive CGR/IG/IGR values and persistent low-order capabilities across rounds. The results are metric- and generator-specific; higher-order emergence and post-composition growth were unavailable, the IE trend was negative, and comparable growth did not consistently beat baselines across phases.”
