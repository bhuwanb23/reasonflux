## Failure taxonomy

1. **Scientific failure/null:** frozen comparison ties, adverse contrasts, or criteria fail. Keep as observed.
2. **Unavailable by design/coverage:** required component skills or metric labels are absent. Report unavailable, not zero effect or support.
3. **Software/reporting failure:** preserve the failed attempt, describe exposure, and do not present rerun as independent confirmation.
4. **External-validity failure:** synthetic generator success does not transfer to ARC; bounded DSL coverage limits interpretation.
5. **Statistical fragility:** few seeds/clusters, deterministic streams, near-zero variance, and multiple secondary contrasts constrain inference.

No new experiments were run for this failure analysis. Sources are linked in the phase-specific packages and in `source_manifest.json`.
