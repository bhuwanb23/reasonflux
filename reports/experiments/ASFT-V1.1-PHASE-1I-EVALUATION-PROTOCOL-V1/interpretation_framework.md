# Phase 1I.7 — Frozen Interpretation Framework

These rules are fixed before outcome access and apply across ASFT v1.1 confirmatory/replication experiments.

1. **Metric increase ≠ theory validation.** A score moving establishes metric behavior only unless independently validated against the construct and outcomes claimed.
2. **Tie = no demonstrated advantage.** A tie is not an ASFT success. Call practical equivalence only when the registered interval is inside SOI with adequate independent-unit precision. An exact point tie or zero variance alone is not enough.
3. **Unavailable ≠ zero.** Missing endpoint, undefined denominator, absent eligible mechanism, masked metric, failed integrity gate, or out-of-policy missingness is unavailable—not a zero, failure, or pass.
4. **Ablation effect ≠ universal necessity.** A removal effect is local to the tested implementation, tasks, budget, endpoint, and activation; null or negative effects are allowed.
5. **Synthetic success ≠ external validity.** Scope remains the tested generator/domain until independent R2/R3/R4 evidence supports generalization.
6. **Correlation ≠ causation.** Predictor association, even if preregistered, does not establish causal mechanism without an identified intervention/assignment and valid contrast.
7. **Capability score ≠ capability growth** until Phase 7 validation is passed against independent behavior, breadth, persistence, and transfer targets. Score movement, object count, and behavioral improvement remain separate.
8. **IG ≠ intelligence** until Phase 8 validation is passed against independent, behavior-based learning/competence outcomes with internal scores excluded from target selection/scoring. Current Phase 14 reviews do not establish this validation.
9. **Implementation success ≠ causal support.** A functioning module, contract pass, constructed skill object, or passing smoke test proves implementation behavior only.
10. **Activation precedes attribution.** Identical decisions, absent eligible opportunities, or dormant components produce non-informative/unavailable mechanism evidence, not a win or null contribution.
11. **ARC failure ≠ theory failure; ARC failure ≠ success.** Bounded coverage or budget failure can limit what mechanisms were tested; it remains a negative result for that implementation and cannot be narrated as success.
12. **Proximate cause ≠ proven root cause.** A plausible representation, memory, frequency, or candidate-pool explanation needs a prospective discriminating test.
13. **No endpoint substitution or phase pooling.** Distinct endpoints, denominators, comparator implementations, and accounting versions stay separate unless an estimand was prospectively harmonized.
14. **No post-outcome reinterpretation.** Hypothesis, endpoint, SOI, thresholds, split, budgets, comparator, exclusion rules, or result-state rules cannot be altered after outcome access. Corrections remain versioned and require independent data for new claims.
15. **Exploratory remains exploratory.** E1/E2 observations can motivate a new preregistered study but do not count as E3/E4 support.
16. **Negative/null/unavailable results remain visible.** Neither a failed gate nor an unavailable endpoint may be erased, relabeled, or omitted from the experiment report.

## Language discipline

Use “observed,” “computed,” “associated,” “not demonstrated,” “contradicted under the registered estimand,” “inconclusive,” “unavailable,” and “non-informative” precisely. Do not use “proves,” “caused,” “emerged,” “generalized,” “capability growth,” or “intelligence growth” unless the matching validation and replication gates passed.
