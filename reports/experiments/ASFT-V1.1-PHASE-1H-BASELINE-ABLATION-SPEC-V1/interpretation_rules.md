# Phase 1H.8 — Frozen Interpretation Rules

1. **Tie = no demonstrated advantage.** Call it an equivalence tie only when the CI lies inside the preregistered SOI region; a wide CI spanning zero is inconclusive.
2. **Unavailable ≠ zero.** Zero opportunity, missing denominator, masked-by-design metric, or failed integrity gate remains unavailable and cannot be imputed as a failure or pass.
3. **Metric increase ≠ theory validation.** A growth/composite score changing proves metric behavior, not construct validity, behavior improvement, or causal theory.
4. **Synthetic success ≠ external validity.** Claims remain within the generator/task representation unless independent external outcomes validate extension.
5. **Ablation effect is local.** It supports contribution under the tested implementation, population, endpoint, and budgets—not universal necessity.
6. **Component presence ≠ necessity.** An ablation may have no measurable, negative, positive, or unavailable effect; none is predetermined.
7. **Identical choices = non-informative selection comparison.** Do not call a policy win when all comparators choose the same candidates/actions.
8. **Unmatched opportunities/pools = attribution limit.** Report per-opportunity and per-attempt estimates, but do not use an unmatched net score as clean causal evidence.
9. **Oracle is not a competitor.** It is an offline ceiling only; an ASFT win/loss against an oracle is not a deployable comparison.
10. **No baseline drift.** Family, subtype, data access, budgets, candidate eligibility, and scoring are frozen before outcomes; changes require a new version and independent confirmatory data.
11. **No endpoint shopping.** Freeze primary/secondary endpoints, multiplicity, and joint success rules. Do not promote a favorable secondary after a primary miss.
12. **No phase pooling without common estimands.** Phase 13B top-k transfer utility, 13C net value, 13D transfer utility, 13E joint C_t/task utility, 13F joint task utility/CGR, and 13G accuracy are distinct; do not average them into an overall ASFT win.
13. **A proximate explanation is not a proven root cause.** Representation, memory, candidate pressure, and implementation can be plausible causes without being experimentally isolated.
14. **Selection and activation are outcomes too.** Report who had a chance to act, which choices diverged, and which components actually fired before interpreting performance.
