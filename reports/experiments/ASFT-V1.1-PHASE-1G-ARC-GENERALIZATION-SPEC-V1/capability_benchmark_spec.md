# Phase 1G.8 — Capability Benchmark Specification

Capability is measured from independent task behavior following Phase 1E, not C_t/CGR/IG or internal capability objects. A candidate capability must be persistent, generalize across held-out instances/partitions, support transfer when claimed, and produce measurable utility under matched budgets.

## Required outputs

- Number of candidate, validated, persistent, and rejected capability units.
- Per-unit and aggregate persistence/retention, task-family breadth, transfer, and net utility with uncertainty and denominators.
- Capability formation and behavioral benefit are separate outcomes; a group object without external task utility is not a validated capability.
- Phase 1E behavioral gate applies: minimum independent tasks, above-baseline external behavior, generality, delayed retention, and positive utility. Exact family-level N/thresholds are calibrated for ARC development and frozen before confirmation.

If an ARC skill/capability lacks the Phase 1E minimum behavioral denominator (e.g. 20 held-out opportunities for a unit) or cannot meet retention/generalization tests, mark capability `unavailable` or `inconclusive`; do not treat zero internal capability objects as zero external capability. Capability is neither accuracy nor intelligence.

**Activation:** at least one unit passes the full external behavior gate. **Not activated:** adequate behavioral opportunities exist but none pass. **Inconclusive/unavailable:** insufficient or absent data. No CGM score can substitute for the behavior gate.
