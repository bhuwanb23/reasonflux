# 14I-F — Interpretation Validity

## Question

Could scientifically plausible results be narrated as claims stronger than the data warrant?

### High-risk interpretation errors

1. **Metric inflation:** positive C_t/CGR/IG/IGR is not automatically capability or intelligence growth. In 13E one capability object persists, while IE trends downward.
2. **Mechanism-to-theory leap:** recurrence, skill construction, or successful reuse does not validate the whole ASFT causal theory. Transfer→capability growth is unestablished; capability→intelligence is unvalidated.
3. **Null-to-universal leap:** policy ties in a low-pressure candidate pool do not prove IDT/SCT/SCV never matter. They do establish no advantage in those frozen comparisons.
4. **ARC-to-theory leap:** 13G's bounded implementation failed upstream. “Representation is the only cause” is not proven, and “ARC disproves ASFT” is not supported.
5. **Operator-to-semantic leap:** fixed DSL program families are not semantic ARC categories; full-DSL demonstration fit is not test-task accuracy.
6. **Proxy-to-compute leap:** simulator step-cost reductions do not establish wall-clock, FLOPs, or search-compute reductions.
7. **Cross-phase concatenation:** positive facts from different protocols cannot be linked into one causal chain without an end-to-end test.
8. **Alternative suppression:** memory/retrieval is strong in 13C but not universally superior; distinct B2 baselines must remain distinct.

## Wording discipline

Use “observed,” “constructed,” “associated,” “within this synthetic cohort,” “not established,” and “unavailable.” Reserve “caused,” “generalized,” “capability growth,” and “intelligence growth” for claims with independent support for those constructs.

## Verdict

**High risk overall; critical for claims about intelligence or a general ASFT theory.** Interpretation should preserve positive synthetic observations without inflating them and preserve the ARC implementation failure without turning it into a general falsification.
