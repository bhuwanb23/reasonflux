# 14I-B — Construct Validity

## Question

Do the operational measures correspond to the theoretical concepts named in the claims?

| Construct / measure | What is actually measured | Threat and safe interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Invariant Strength (IS) | Frequency × consistency × generality, with generality bounded by observed program/task buckets | May measure recurrence/fit to the fixed ontology rather than usefulness or abstract invariance. 13B relationship is based on about 4.4 promoted candidates per seed; corrected 13D relationship is near zero/non-positive. |
| Skill Creation Value (SCV) | A frozen composite of reuse, abstraction, transfer, and cost components | 13C association with future value is positive, but frequency and p_reuse are numerically stronger; all confirmation candidates are selected. Incremental selection value is unestablished. |
| Transfer | Correct applications to generated, covered tasks and phase-specific transfer utility | Supported narrowly; not open-world generalization. `91/91` cross-family is conditional on reflection/rotation skills and the attempted cases. |
| Reasoning-cost reduction | Simulator reasoning-step proxy | Not wall time, FLOPs, solver search, or compute savings. |
| Capability growth / CGR | Change in repository-defined capability score | 13E's score grows while one object persists; it is not a direct count of new capabilities or demonstrated breadth. 13D results differ. |
| IG / IGR / IE | Weighted internal composite, difference, and experience-normalized ratio | No independent intelligence criterion. Positive IG/IGR are formula outputs; IE trends down in 13E. |
| ARC task family | Fixed DSL operator labels | Not semantic ARC taxonomy. Demo-fit coverage is not task-level test accuracy. “Unsupported” is implementation-defined. |
| Skill emergence | Candidate-to-skill object construction and storage | Evidence for represented reflection/rotation skill instances, not unprompted or general emergence. |

## Verdict

**High risk overall; critical for intelligence-growth claims.** Prefer operational language (“score,” “stored skill object,” “successful reuse”) unless an independent validation supports the broader construct label. Report formula and denominator beside every construct-level claim.
