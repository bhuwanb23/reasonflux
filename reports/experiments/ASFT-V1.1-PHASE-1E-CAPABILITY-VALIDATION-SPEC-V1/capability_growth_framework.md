# Phase 1E.5 — Capability Growth Framework

## Behavioral growth, not score growth

The primary external object is the External Behavioral Capability Score (EBCS) from the independent A–F battery. For a panel/system, define:

`ΔEBCS = EBCS_future − EBCS_disjoint_baseline`.

A positive `ΔEBCS` is necessary but not alone sufficient for a capability-growth claim: it must exceed the smallest effect of interest under a same-budget comparison, persist at the delayed retention test, and be supported by transfer/generalization or breadth behavior. Internal C_t/CGR/IG changes are predictors/diagnostics only; they do not enter the definition, formula, threshold, or success label for ΔEBCS.

## Valid growth patterns

- Increased success on novel tasks within a validated skill family, with retention and positive utility.
- More independent task families passing the family-level gate.
- Higher cross-family transfer or compositional success on untouched tasks.
- Adaptation gain retained after a no-refresh interval.

## Invalid growth claims

- C_t/CGR/IG rises without an external behavior change.
- Capability-object count or size increases without task success.
- Improvement is limited to repeated test tasks or exact answer retrieval.
- Immediate performance rises but vanishes after delay.
- More attempts, memory, compute, or task exposure are given to one arm.
- The same test tasks or labels are used to tune the predictor or define the target.

## Growth categories

Report continuous ΔEBCS, family-wise changes, validated breadth delta, retention, composition/adaptation outcomes, and net utility. A system can show growth in one subtype while regressing in another; do not compress such profiles into CGR alone. Use the SOI and multiplicity plan in `capability_metrics.md`.
