# Component-Importance Assessment

## IDT: partial, endpoint-specific contribution

Phase 13D shows positive ASFT-minus-no-invariants transfer utility in covered cells; Phase 13E shows lower CGR when IDT is removed. But Phase 13B's transfer-aware score ties frequency/no-transfer and Phase 13F's no-IDT arm has slightly higher task utility while ASFT has higher CGR. Phase 13F frequency-only also ties ASFT on utility/CGR. Verdict: some local contribution, not demonstrated as necessary or uniquely valuable.

## SCT and SCV: necessity not identified by current pools

Phase 13C selects all 44 eligible confirmation candidate rows under SCT, ALWAYS_CREATE, and RANDOM_CREATE. Calibration has 22/22 SCV10>0 and random-create probability 1.0. Phase 13F selects four of four candidates under ASFT, no-SCV, and random-SCV. These are informative failures of differentiation in the tested pools; they do not prove the benchmark universally lacks ranking leverage. SCV is descriptively associated with value in Phase 13C, but frequency and p_reuse are at least as predictive and policy-level superiority fails.

## Persistence versus lifecycle

Phase 13D ASFT exceeds no-persistence on its corrected transfer endpoint (`+.017018`), suggesting a local role for retaining skills. Lifecycle toggles in 13C and 13F show zero primary difference. Persistence of useful skills and retirement/reactivation policy are distinct mechanisms; evidence for one should not be assigned to the other.

## Transfer and capability metrics

Phase 13F transfer removal reduces some CGM scores but leaves ASFT task utility below the no-transfer comparator. A capability-layer removal changes CGR without changing task utility. These are direct warnings that growth metrics and task behavior are separate outcome families.

## Composition

Observed composition is heterogeneous: one successful Phase 13C rotation→reflection case; unavailable components in 13D; negative incremental value and no new capability count in 13E; positive composed-task utility in one 13F stream. Composition's importance is unresolved beyond those endpoints.

**Overall:** no single ASFT component is established as universally necessary. Strongest local positive component evidence is for invariants/persistence on selected synthetic transfer/growth endpoints; strongest null evidence is SCV/SCT/lifecycle in pools where selected sets/actions do not diverge.
