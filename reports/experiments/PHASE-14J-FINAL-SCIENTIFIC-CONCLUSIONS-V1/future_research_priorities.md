# Future Research Priorities

These priorities are future directions, not new experiments or metric changes made in Phase 14J.

1. **Representation and protocol alignment:** specify a broader ARC-compatible representation and trace grammar, with the discovery and scoring budgets aligned to a preregistered protocol. Keep demo-fit coverage distinct from exact task accuracy.
2. **Selection pressure and competing mechanisms:** use candidate pools that require real positive/negative ranking and creation choices. Compare ASFT against frequency-only, no-transfer, episodic memory/retrieval, and compression controls using clear, non-interchangeable baseline definitions.
3. **Capability validation:** evaluate capability claims against independent behavioral breadth, persistence, and transfer outcomes. Separate capability-object counts, portfolio scores, task performance, and growth metrics.
4. **External evaluation:** replicate beyond the current generator and operation vocabulary, using independent datasets and out-of-generator tasks. Treat ARC as confirmatory only after the adapter and its protocol have been specified and validated independently.
