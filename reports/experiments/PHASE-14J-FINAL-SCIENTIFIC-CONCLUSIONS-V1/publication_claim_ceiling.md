# Publication Claim Ceiling — ASFT v0.1

> **In controlled synthetic grid-task environments, the implemented ASFT pipeline repeatedly detects operation-level structures aligned with the generator, constructs reflection/rotation skill objects, and records successful reuse with reductions in a simulator reasoning-step cost proxy. The evidence does not demonstrate transfer-aware ranking or SCT selection superiority, a causal transfer-to-capability-growth effect, validated intelligence growth, or ARC generalization. Repository-defined capability and intelligence metrics rise in one synthetic cohort but lack independent construct validation. The bounded ARC adapter has very low task/program coverage and does not instantiate the downstream mechanism chain.**

## Scope guardrails

- “Invariant” means an operation-level recurring structure within the tested synthetic vocabulary; it does not mean a general/open-ended concept.
- “Skill” means a stored reflection/rotation object created from validated synthetic candidates; it does not prove SCT selection superiority or broad emergence.
- “Transfer” means successful reuse in tested generated tasks; the reported cost reduction is a simulator step-cost proxy, not wall time, FLOPs, or search reduction.
- “Capability growth” and “intelligence growth” must be attributed to repository-defined metrics unless independently validated.
- ARC counts are full-DSL demonstration-fit coverage, not task accuracy or semantic taxonomy. ARC failed to activate downstream mechanisms under a bounded adapter; it neither validates nor broadly falsifies the theory.

Any stronger claim would exceed the current evidence.
