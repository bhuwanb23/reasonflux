# ASFT v1.1 Phase 2G — ARC Foundation Readiness Report

**Phase ID:** `ASFT-V1.1-PHASE-2G`  
**Package:** `ASFT-V1.1-PHASE-2G-ARC-FOUNDATION-READINESS-V1`  
**Review scope:** integrate Phases 2A–2F. No new ARC science, search, task scoring, test-output access, or representation experiment.

## Executive decision

**Phase 2 exit: PARTIAL. ARC mechanism-level testing: BLOCKED.**

Phases 2A–2F provide meaningful diagnostic infrastructure for the existing bounded R1 pipeline: representation is documented; test-blind found-fit coverage is measurable; Phase 2C search budgets and replay are auditable; trace generation/validity/success and recurrence are measurable; invariant attrition is stage-localized. However, the alternative representation framework is not implemented, the current invariant pipeline receives too few successful recurring traces to test meaningful discovery, validation/FDR are not independently established, and no eligible skills, transfer units, or capabilities exist. Therefore the stronger claim that ARC failures can now be attributed among representation, search, and downstream mechanisms is not yet warranted.

## Phase 2A–2F integrated findings

- **Representation:** Phase 13G's ten whole-grid operations are exactly documented. The DSL is narrow; no-fit remains bounded found-fit failure, not semantic impossibility. R2–R7 alternatives are specified in Phase 2F, not built.
- **Coverage:** Phase 2B/2C test-output-blind measured fit coverage at 2/1,000 for cap3 and 10/1,000 at the finite fullscan. Phase 2C cap sensitivity (2, 5, 10, 10) shows search budget affects coverage within R1. Coverage is not accuracy.
- **Search integrity:** Phase 2C B1–B6 parity and deterministic replay passed. Phase 13G historical discovery nevertheless used cap10 despite configured cap3, and first-fit/parameter inference remain confounds in historical attribution.
- **Traces:** Phase 2D reconstructed and structurally validated 4,000 current task-condition traces; success counts were 2/5/10/10. Historical 600 traces pass retrospective structure checks, but no generation-time validator was established. Exact solution-program/context support maxed at one in Phase 2D; recurring generic trace-control n-grams were separately measured in Phase 2E.
- **Invariants:** Phase 2E made recurrence and miner stages measurable. At current support5, zero patterns are expected from a maximum support of three. Relaxed support produces generic control-flow patterns, which fail candidate-generation filters. This does not establish that meaningful invariants do not exist or that the miner fails on adequate semantic inputs.
- **Alternatives:** Phase 2F specifies paired budgets/endpoints, HCC, and attribution rules. It did not execute a comparison. All 1,000 Phase 2C IDs have already been reviewed; reruns on them are diagnostic only. A confirmatory comparison requires a new authorized untouched common set.

## What Phase 2 demonstrates

✅ The bounded R1 representation and its exact found-fit coverage are documented.  
✅ Test-output-blind coverage and budget sensitivity can be measured.  
✅ Config/runtime/log parity, actual trials, and deterministic replay can be audited for the new Phase 2C runner.  
✅ Trace emission, structural validity, success yield, support, and invariant pipeline attrition are measurable.  
✅ Some failures in the existing R1 diagnostic chain are localized to bounded coverage, successful-trace scarcity, recurrence, and frozen support filtering.  
✅ The representation hypothesis has a falsifiable comparison framework and explicit alternatives.

## What Phase 2 does not demonstrate

❌ ASFT works on ARC.  
❌ Representation is the primary bottleneck or that a richer representation will work.  
❌ Meaningful invariants are present—or absent—in ARC.  
❌ Skills emerge from ARC traces.  
❌ ASFT transfer succeeds on an independent ARC split.  
❌ Capability or intelligence emerges on ARC.  
❌ Coverage gain implies test accuracy or generalization.  
❌ Phase 3/SCT confirmatory evaluation is ready or authorized.

## Residual gates before mechanism testing

1. Implement and hash R2–R7 plus HCC; preflight the common trace and downstream adapters.
2. Obtain a new authorized, untouched common task set; preserve test-output seals and avoid the historically reused holdout.
3. Freeze independent task-family annotations, `δ_coverage` and activation margins, statistical plan, and all endpoints before outcomes.
4. Demonstrate high-coverage HCC plumbing without treating it as ARC evidence.
5. Supply enough successful, diverse discovery traces to meet frozen support rules; if this fails, report readiness blocked rather than lowering thresholds post hoc.
6. Establish independent invariant validation and an FDR/false-discovery method; only then test skill and transfer eligibility.
7. Re-review config/runtime/log parity, provenance, split integrity, and evaluator separation before any confirmatory authorization.

## Final statement

> **Phase 2 has made failures in the current bounded R1 diagnostic pipeline substantially more diagnosable, but it has not yet made the representation-versus-downstream mechanism question empirically answerable. Phase 2 exit is PARTIAL; ARC mechanism testing remains BLOCKED.**
