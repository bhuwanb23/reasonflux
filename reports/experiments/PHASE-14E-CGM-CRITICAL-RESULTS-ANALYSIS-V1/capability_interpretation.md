# Capability Interpretation

## What Phase 13E formed

All ten ASFT seeds contain the same `cap-cross-domain` capability ID at rounds 1–4. It is accepted/validated and persists. Its membership grows from reflection+rotation skills to include a composed skill at round 3; its state progresses from developing to established to core. This is credible evidence that the system can represent and maintain a validated skill aggregate under the synthetic protocol.

## What “growth” means in this record

There is exactly one capability object in every seed-round row. The capability count does not grow after round 1: `new_capabilities` is 1 at round 1 and 0 in rounds 2–4. The measured C_t can still rise because it is the mean CapU-like score over active capabilities, and each capability score is a weighted combination of member-skill utility, observed transfer score, coverage, and composition bonus. Member skills and evidence change, so score growth is not equivalent to new capability formation.

## Boundary

No higher-order/emergent-type capability occurrence is accepted; the cross-domain capability is an aggregate, not a demonstrated independent abstract competence. Phase 13E supports **capability representation, persistence, and score evolution** more strongly than capability emergence in the theory-level sense. It does not establish that the score equals a transferable behavioral competence outside the synthetic generator.
