# Threats-to-Validity Assessment

| ID | Severity | Threat | Consequence / treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| CGM-01 | High | C_t is a weighted aggregation of skill utility, transfer, coverage, and composition. | Score increase can track skill accumulation/evidence rather than an independent capability construct. Report metric growth separately from capability-count growth. |
| CGM-02 | High | No accepted higher-order emergent-type capability; one cross-domain object persists. | Capability representation is not equivalent to emergent abstract competence. |
| CGM-03 | High | Cross-phase CGR changes sign: 13E `+0.2946`, corrected 13D `-0.060`. | Robustness across generator/context is not established. Do not pool unlike cohorts. |
| CGM-04 | High | TS→CGR is negative/uncertain in 13E (`r=-.503`) and positive descriptive in 13D (`r≈.483`). | Transfer-to-growth causality or reliable direction cannot be inferred. |
| CGM-05 | High | IG/IGR are constructed from internal growth deltas; IE is `IG/experiences`. | Positive outputs validate implementation, not intelligence construct validity; negative IE slope is a ratio trend. |
| CGM-06 | Medium-high | Mean IG/IGR averages conceal initialization and non-monotone rounds. | Show per-round path alongside seed-level means; round 1 uses zero prior state. |
| CGM-07 | High | Composition gain is `-.1429`; no 4th-vs-2nd-round capability-count growth in 10/10. | No composition-as-capability-generator claim for this cohort. |
| CGM-08 | High | Baseline comparisons vary by phase/comparator and utility endpoint. | Avoid universal superiority claims; state protocol and metric for each contrast. |
| CGM-09 | Medium-high | C_t→future-utility association is small (`r=.0498`) within 10 seed clusters and same generator. | Preserve preregistered directional pass but limit interpretation; no causal or external prediction. |
| CGM-10 | High | No independent behavioral or external intelligence criterion. | IG/IGR/IE cannot be interpreted as general intelligence growth. |
| CGM-11 | High | Phase 13G generated no valid ARC capability units. | ARC growth is unavailable/not demonstrated, not a measured negative effect. |
| CGM-12 | Medium | CCG uses four known synthetic task families; coverage is generator-defined. | Positive CCG does not establish broad semantic coverage. |

**Overall:** formula validity and internal computability are stronger than construct validity. The serious risk is a correct implementation of a score being interpreted as evidence for a broader theoretical construct.
