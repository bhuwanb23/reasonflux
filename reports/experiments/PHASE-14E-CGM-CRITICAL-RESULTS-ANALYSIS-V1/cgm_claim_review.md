# CGM Claim Review

| Claim | Evidence and counter-evidence | Review verdict |
|---|---|---|
| CGM-1 — capabilities emerge from skills | The 13E pipeline accepts a cross-domain capability assembled from reflection/rotation skills and persists it 10/10 seeds. It remains one object; no accepted higher-order emergent-type occurrences exist. | **Partial:** capability representation/aggregation supported; strong emergence not shown. |
| CGM-2 — capabilities grow over time | C_t is monotone in 10/10 Phase 13E seeds; mean CGR `0.2946` (95% CI `[0.2857,0.3036]`). But capability count is exactly one at each round; its score/member set changes. Corrected 13D mean CGR is `-0.060`. | **Partial:** score growth in one synthetic cohort; not robust capability-count growth across phases. |
| CGM-3 — growth predicts performance | Preregistered 13E C_t→next-round task utility is positive `r=0.0498`, cluster CI `[0.0163,0.0922]`, and passes the frozen directional criterion. Effect is tiny (`r²≈.0025`), same-generator, 10 clusters. The separate TS→CGR result is `r=-0.5027` with CI spanning zero. | **Limited evidence:** weak within-cohort prediction, no substantial, causal, transfer-mediated, or external prediction claim. |
| CGM-4 — intelligence growth can be measured | IG/IGR/IE compute deterministically, but IG is a weighted composite of CGR/TG/UG/CCG/CompG, IGR is its change, and IE divides IG by experiences. | **Not established as intelligence:** internal indices exist; construct validity does not. |
| CGM-5 — capability growth proxies intelligence growth | C_t and IG share internal skill and growth ingredients. No independent external intelligence criterion or validated proxy relation exists. | **Not supported.** |

## Scope correction

Do not describe the 13E future-prediction result as simply “failed”: the frozen statistic passes its directional gate. Conversely, do not elevate that pass into a robust CGM theory result. The observed coefficient is near zero in magnitude and lives inside one synthetic cohort. The narrower conclusion is that **the score has a weak within-cohort association with next-round task utility under the preregistered pairing**.
