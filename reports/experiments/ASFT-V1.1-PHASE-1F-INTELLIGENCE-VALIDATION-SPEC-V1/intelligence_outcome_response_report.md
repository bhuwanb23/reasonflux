# Phase 1F — Outcome-Response Audit

The contract tests feed constructed confidence intervals and slope bounds to the decision code to demonstrate that the benchmark can return every intended state:

| Constructed case | Incremental ΔMAE interval | IG slope interval | Code status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win | `[0.04, 0.08]` | `[0.01, 0.10]` | `ig_wins` |
| Equivalence | `[-0.01, 0.01]` | `[-0.01, 0.01]` | `equivalent_tie` |
| Loss/inverse | `[-0.08, -0.03]` | `[-0.10, -0.01]` | `ig_loses` |
| Uncertain | `[-0.02, 0.04]` | `[-0.01, 0.03]` | `inconclusive` |
| Integrity failure | unavailable | unavailable | `unavailable` |

A win requires both meaningful positive held-out error reduction and positive IG direction. A metric that predicts in the wrong direction cannot be called an intelligence win. Separate contract scenarios verify that the claim may survive only with replicated learning plus an additional external win, and that adequately powered replicated equivalence/loss can invoke retirement.

**Evidence boundary:** these are hand-constructed unit-test values. They are not estimated from IG, not generated by task rankings, and not task or model outcomes. They verify decision paths only; they provide no evidence that IG actually wins, ties, loses, or should be retired.
