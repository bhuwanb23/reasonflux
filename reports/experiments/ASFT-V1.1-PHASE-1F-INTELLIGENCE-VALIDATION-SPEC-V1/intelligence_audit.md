# Phase 1F.1 — Historical Intelligence-Metric Audit

**Scope:** read-only audit of Phase 13E and Phase 14E/14H/14I/14J records. No historical experiment was rerun or changed.

## Executive judgment

The saved record establishes that the repository's intelligence formula is **computable** and produces positive aggregate IG/IGR values in one ten-seed synthetic cohort. It does not establish that IG measures intelligence, that intelligence grew, or that IG predicts future learning/adaptation/competence beyond simpler variables. Phase 14I classifies construct validity as high risk and critical for intelligence-growth claims; Phase 14J scores the intelligence-growth interpretation as not established.

## What Phase 13E observed

The confirmatory formula was `IG = .35*CGR + .25*TG + .20*UG + .15*CCG + .05*CompG`. IGR is the round-to-round difference in IG. IE is `IG / experiences`, where experiences are 32 discovery traces plus cumulative held-out tasks.

Across ten synthetic ReasonFlux-grid seeds:

| Metric | Mean | 95% CI | What the number means |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| IG | 0.2294 | [0.2245, 0.2343] | Mean output of the frozen internal composite. |
| IGR | 0.00904 | [0.00903, 0.00906] | Mean of internal round-to-round differences, including the initial zero-state transition. |
| IE | 0.0043 | [0.0041, 0.0044] | Internal IG divided by cumulative experience. |
| IE OLS slope | −0.00394 | [−0.00421, −0.00367] | Decline in the internal ratio over rounds, not measured human-like intelligence efficiency. |

Round-average IG was approximately `.6573, .0801, .1442, .0362`; IGR was `.6573, −.5772, .0641, −.1080`. Thus, the positive across-round mean does not mean IG/IGR increased every round. A large initial transition from the zero prior state contributes strongly.

The formula components—CGR, transfer score, utility growth, capability coverage, and composition growth—are repository-defined internal quantities. No independent intelligence labels, externally measured future learning curve, adaptation target, or held-out domain acquisition outcome was used to validate IG.

## What later audits concluded

- **Phase 14E:** IG/IGR/IE are reproducibly computed; the intelligence interpretation needs an independent criterion, evidence beyond shared formula components, and validation beyond the generator.
- **Phase 14H:** Capability Growth→Intelligence Growth is unvalidated; positive indices in one cohort do not establish the causal chain.
- **Phase 14I:** construct-validity risk is high overall and critical for intelligence-growth claims; positive IG/IGR are formula outputs, and IE falls.
- **Phase 14J:** “IG measures intelligence growth” is not established. No independent intelligence criterion validates the label.

The v1.0 criticism—that IG may be a mathematical composite of internal metrics—is consistent with the source definitions and audit verdicts. It is a construct-validity concern, not evidence that the formula fails to execute.

## Missing evidence

No existing Phase 13/14 result establishes that IG predicts an independent future learning curve, adaptation recovery, few-shot competence acquisition, novel-environment success, cross-domain learning, or generalization retention after controlling for past performance, experience, capability score, memory, and transfer. No replication of such a test exists. Positive internal metric movement alone cannot fill that gap.

## Audit conclusion

> Phase 13E demonstrates positive mean values of an internally defined IG/IGR pathway in one synthetic cohort, alongside a declining internal IE ratio and non-monotone round trajectories. Phase 14E/14H/14I/14J reject treating those numbers as validated intelligence growth. Phase 1F therefore freezes an external behavior target first; IG enters only later as a candidate predictor. If no independent predictive value survives comparison and replication, the intelligence label is retired and IG is renamed as an internal composite.
