# Alternative-Explanation Analysis: Memory and Retrieval

## Why memory remains a live explanation

Phase 13C's raw MEMORY_ONLY arm reports primary net value `1.325`, compared with SCT `0.345`. MEMORY_ONLY applies in 440/440 eligible opportunities and records 418 correct applications; SCT applies in 223/440 and records 201 correct positive-credit applications. This is the strongest direct counterexample to a claim that ASFT's skill-creation policy is needed to explain all reuse benefit in that protocol.

The competing path is:

`experience → stored episode/example → retrieval → reuse/transfer`

It can generate successful reuse without showing that abstract invariant discovery or SCV-based selection is superior.

## What the memory result does not establish

- The policies are not application-count matched. Net value combines frequency of match/application, correctness, and costs; it does not isolate per-attempt retrieval quality from abstraction quality.
- “MEMORY_ONLY” is raw trace-exemplar retrieval, not the same mechanism as every later B2 baseline.
- Phase 13D B2_MEMORY_ONLY, Phase 13E skill-storage-only, and Phase 13F B2_MEMORY_SKILL_STORE have different implementations, task streams, and decision metrics.
- Later phases sometimes favor ASFT on their frozen endpoints, while other comparisons are mixed. They do not invalidate the Phase 13C result or establish memory dominance universally.
- ARC B2's 1/200 transfer success is a single episodic-memory task success; it is not learned skill transfer and not an estimate of a general memory advantage.

## Judgment

Memory/retrieval is a **surviving competing mechanism** that the cross-phase evidence has not fully separated from skill abstraction and transfer. It is too strong to say “memory explains everything,” but equally too strong to claim the observed benefit uniquely depends on ASFT's invariant/skill chain. The saved record supports a mixed and comparator-specific verdict, not a pooled memory-vs-ASFT effect.
