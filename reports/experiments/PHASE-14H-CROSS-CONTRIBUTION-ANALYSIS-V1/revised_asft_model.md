# Revised ASFT Model

## Original model

`Experience → Invariant Discovery → Skill Formation → Transfer → Capability Growth → Intelligence Growth`

The original model requires all transitions to hold. The saved cross-phase evidence does not establish that conjunction.

## Evidence-weighted synthetic model

```text
Controlled synthetic experience
        ↓
Recurring operation-level trace patterns
        ↓
Reusable reflection/rotation skill objects
        ↓
Some successful reuse and simulator step-cost reduction
        ↓
? Capability-score growth (transfer link inconsistent / unestablished)
        ↓
? Intelligence growth (construct validity unestablished)
```

The first three transitions have narrow operational support in designed synthetic tasks. They do not establish open-ended discovery, SCT selection superiority, broad transfer, or a complete same-cohort causal chain.

## Surviving alternative pathway

```text
Stored experience → episode retrieval → reuse/transfer
```

Phase 13C's raw memory-only baseline is strong, though not application-count matched. Memory cannot be ruled out as part of the explanation; it is not established as a universal replacement for skills.

## ARC adapter pathway

```text
ARC task → low coverage under bounded DSL → sparse successful traces → no invariant candidates
                                                               ↳ downstream skills/transfer/capabilities unavailable
```

This describes the tested implementation only. The saved discovery code uses ten candidates although the frozen config declares three, so even the observed fit yield is not fully budget-conformant. No claim is made that a corrected or broader ARC representation would validate the downstream links.

## Revised scientific claim

> Across controlled synthetic tasks, ASFT detects recurring operation-level trace patterns, constructs reusable reflection/rotation skill objects, and records successful reuse with reduced simulator reasoning-step cost. These results support a narrow synthetic subchain from recurring experience through skill reuse, but do not establish open-ended invariant discovery, SCT selection superiority, general transfer, or a causal transfer-to-capability-growth effect. Repository-defined capability and intelligence-growth indices rise in one synthetic cohort, but capability-to-intelligence validity is untested. The bounded ARC adapter fails upstream at task/program coverage and produces no invariant/skill/capability chain; this is an implementation/external-validity failure, not a direct falsification of the broader theories.
