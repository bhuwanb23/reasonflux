# Phase 2I — Decision Rules

## Outcome classes

| Outcome | Rule | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Nonzero invariant population produced** | At least one unique invariant candidate passes frozen generation and outcome-blind structural eligibility; counts, lineage, family evidence, duplicates, and rejection reasons are auditable. | Necessary evidence of candidate substrate. Does **not** authorize Phase 3B; reopen Phase 3A-R to test actual pool pressure, feature lineage, endpoint, seal, and power. |
| **P2 — Zero under both registered arms** | R1 and R3 both yield zero accepted candidates under unchanged thresholds, with trace/pipeline controls passing. | F2 remains for the tested pipelines; consider further upstream remediation or a formal F3 redesign. Do not lower support post hoc. |
| **P3 — Representation increases upstream coverage but not accepted invariants** | R3 improves demo-fit/trace/pattern stages, but no invariant passes generation/eligibility. | Candidate substrate remains absent; report the exact bottleneck, no Phase 3 unlock. |
| **P4 — Candidate pipeline/control invalid** | Config/hash mismatch, failed trace schema, family annotation invalid, or HCC cannot demonstrate expected plumbing. | Phase2I execution unavailable; repair implementation without changing the candidate contract, then preregister a fresh development run. |
| **P5 — Alternative objects only** | Patterns/fragments/structures exist but no accepted invariant candidates. | Do not count as P1. F3 redesign discussion only if owner explicitly changes the ranked object and writes a new protocol. |

## Integrity rules

- Preserve support/consistency/family/rejection thresholds unchanged; no quota fill or post-result threshold tuning.
- No test-output access, future utility, transfer labels, Oracle, or family labels derived from selected solver programs.
- Do not select a favorable representation/seed/task subset based on yield. Report all registered conditions.
- No pooling across R1/R3, thresholds, or repeated task conditions to manufacture unique N.
- Candidate yield is not ranking pressure, FNTU, IDT support, or efficacy.

## Phase transition

P1 only triggers **Phase 3A-R re-review**, not Phase 3B directly. Phase 3B remains prohibited until Phase 3A-R's six unlock gates pass. If P2/P3/P5 occurs, Phase 3 remains blocked; an F3 object-layer redesign requires explicit approval and its own benchmark design.
