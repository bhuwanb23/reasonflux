## Phase 2I measurement plan

For R1 and R3 separately, calculate counts after every stage in `candidate_production_protocol.md`, including both raw count and unique canonical count. Report task and family denominators, not just aggregate candidates. Candidate yield is:

`accepted unique invariant candidates / eligible discovery task IDs`

Also report proposals per successful trace, proposal rejection rate by reason, unique candidates per family, candidate source support distribution, and candidate identity overlap between arms. No outcome values are needed or permitted.

## Interpretation boundaries

- **Nonzero patterns are not nonzero accepted invariants.**
- **Nonzero generated candidates are not necessarily structurally qualified or feature-ready.** Keep those counts separate.
- **A candidate yield increase under R3 is a representation effect on the frozen development set**, not transfer efficacy or confirmation.
- **Zero yield under R1 and R3 does not prove invariant candidates are impossible on ARC.** It means the tested, frozen pipelines did not produce them from the permitted data.
