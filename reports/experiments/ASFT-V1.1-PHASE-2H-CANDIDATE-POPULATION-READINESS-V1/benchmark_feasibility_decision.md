# Phase 2H.5 — Benchmark Feasibility Decision

## Feasibility outcomes

| Outcome | Decision | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| F1 — Eligible invariant population exists; proceed toward Phase 3 | **NO** | The audited ARC R1 path has zero accepted candidates, no registry, and no pressure-capable pool. |
| F2 — Current candidate population does not exist; IDT benchmark unavailable | **YES for the current audited invariant path** | Current/support-5 candidate yield is zero; relaxed proposals are rejected and cannot fill the pool. |
| F3 — A different candidate layer is required; redesign Phase 3 | **POSSIBLE, NOT ADOPTED** | Patterns/traces/structures/skills are present or conceivable as objects, but they are not approved R1 candidates. Choosing one changes the research construct. |

## Verdict

> **The current ARC pipeline does not produce a nonzero eligible invariant-candidate population for the Phase 1C R1–R5 benchmark. The RQ1 benchmark is unavailable on the audited ARC substrate. Phase 3 remains blocked.**

This is a statement about the *current bounded ARC pipeline, saved data, and frozen policies*. It is not proof that IDT cannot generate candidates on any data, that the invariant concept is impossible, or that all ARC tasks lack recurring structures. Phase 2G's representation/search attribution remains unresolved; low successful-trace yield, support scarcity, and generic relaxed proposals are upstream blockers.

## What follows

- Do not start Phase 3B, compute power/N, calibrate features, or access a Phase 3D outcome store for this absent population.
- Do not use the synthetic N=40 fixture, rejected proposals, or downstream skills/programs as substitutes.
- Phase 2 may continue with a preregistered substrate/remediation decision—e.g., improve trace yield/representation or explicitly redesign the candidate unit—but should not claim candidate population readiness until eligible nonzero objects are produced under a frozen, outcome-blind rule.
- Any different candidate layer requires explicit F3 approval and a new benchmark specification before generating or scoring its outcomes.
