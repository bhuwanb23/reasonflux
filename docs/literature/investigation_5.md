Final Research Conclusion

If I had to reduce months of ARC and AGI literature into one research thesis, it would be:

Current AI systems learn solutions. Future AGI systems must learn reusable capabilities.

Or even more precisely:

Intelligence may emerge not from solving tasks, but from converting solved tasks into reusable abstractions that improve future reasoning.

This is the strongest research gap that consistently survives all five investigations.
