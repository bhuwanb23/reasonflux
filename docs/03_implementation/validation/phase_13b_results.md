Ranking diagnostics show ASFT, frequency-only and no-transfer produce the **same semantic ranking on all five seeds**: reflection, rotation, counting, grouping. In this benchmark, reflection has substantially higher held-out utility than the other categories; rotation is much lower; counting and grouping have zero measured utility in this setup. The data therefore do not create a choice where transfer evidence changes the ordering relative to intrinsic frequency/strength. The random control is worse, but that does not satisfy the required superiority over the informed baselines.

This diagnosis is now included at per-seed level in `reports/experiments/idt_evidence/table_3_baselines_by_seed.json`. It explains the tie; it does not convert it into a pass.

## Cost-accounting correction made in this review

Macro replay for a reflection/rotation skill on a counting or grouping task previously omitted the target's `count_objects`/`group_by_color` reasoning step, even though `_output_for` still computed that output. This made part of the measured reduction free. `GridSolver` now emits and charges the required family-output step unless the macro itself already contains it. A regression test checks both symmetric cross-family cases; transfer utility falls after the correction. The baseline tie remains.

This repairs that specific accounting omission. The metric is still a step-count cost in a small simulator, not wall-clock/compute cost for a general ARC agent.

## Interpretation limits

1. Tasks and reasoning traces are generated by the same synthetic four-family system. The family, reference transform and operation labels are known to the task generator/solver; this demonstrates behavior on a controlled mechanism, not open-ended ARC discovery.
2. “Expected signature” checks allow a mined operation component to match a multi-operation concept. Treat discovery as component-level synthetic evidence unless and until a separate blinded semantic annotation protocol validates full invariants.
3. The IS–TS correlation population averages 4.4 promoted candidates per seed. Perfect correlations on this small, constructed population are fragile and do not establish general predictive power.
4. Current transfer-discriminative power is absent against frequency-only/no-transfer in this benchmark. Do not claim that transfer-aware invariant ranking is superior on this evidence.
5. No ARC-AGI-2/3 data or submission is included in this Phase 13B run. These results are not external-validity evidence.

## Supplemental EXP-IDT-14 stress test

After the original tie, an explicitly adversarial controlled benchmark was added to test whether transfer-aware ranking can reject a frequent but non-transfer-bearing trace pattern. It adds a no-op `normalize_grid` marker to successful discovery traces in three of four families. The original `synthetic-v1` results and gate were left untouched.

The final EXP-IDT-14 run used seeds 10–14; the per-family-six/per-family-twelve prototypes on seeds 5–9 were exploratory and excluded. On the frozen final setup, ASFT's mean top-1 held-out TU was **0.164866** (SD 0.001100; 95% normal CI 0.163902–0.165831), versus **0.000000** for frequency-only and no-transfer and **0.059557** for random. ASFT strictly won top-1 on every run seed. The no-op candidate had frequency 30 in each seed, above the reflection component (25–27); transfer-aware selected reflection first, while frequency-only and IS-only selected `normalize_grid` first. At top-2, ASFT also leads on mean TU (0.096322 vs 0.082433); from top-3 onward the curves tie.

This is a **supplemental controlled stress-test result**, not confirmation on natural data and not a replacement for the original Phase 13B baseline comparison. The no-op confound was intentionally inserted and the benchmark was designed after seeing the earlier tie; this demonstrates that the ranking mechanism can respond to this known confound in this simulator, but it does not show the same advantage on the original benchmark or on ARC.

## What is required before successful Phase 13B closure

The original `synthetic-v1` strict gate remains failed. To establish a confirmatory result, preregister a new benchmark before inspecting its results, use an independently justified confound/task distribution and untouched task seeds, retain paired per-task outcomes, and report both top-1 and ranking-wide metrics. If transfer-aware ranking still ties on the original or a defensible independent benchmark, preserve the null result and narrow IDT's claim. EXP-IDT-14 does not change the original closure status.
