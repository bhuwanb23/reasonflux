14H Conclusion
Scientific Verdict

The strongest evidence-supported conclusion is:

ASFT successfully demonstrates an end-to-end synthetic pipeline from experience through invariant discovery, skill formation, and transfer. The evidence does not establish that transfer causes capability growth, that capability metrics represent intelligence growth, or that the full mechanism chain operates on ARC.
