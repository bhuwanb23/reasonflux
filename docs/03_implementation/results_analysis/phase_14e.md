14E-14 Revised CGM Claim

Original claim:

Capability growth can be measured and used as a proxy for intelligence growth.

Evidence-supported claim:

ASFT implements a repeatable capability-metric framework that produces positive capability and intelligence trajectories in controlled synthetic environments. However, the current evidence does not establish that these trajectories correspond to robust capability growth, predict future performance, represent intelligence growth, or generalize beyond the synthetic setting.
