Phase 14J: Final Scientific Conclusions
Purpose

Phase 14J is the final scientific verdict of the entire ASFT v0.1 research program.

This phase does not introduce new experiments, new metrics, new analyses, or new interpretations.

Its purpose is to answer:

Given all evidence collected in Phases 11–14, what can be scientifically defended, what cannot be defended, and what is the true contribution of ASFT?

14J-8 Final Research Contribution

The strongest defensible contribution is:

ASFT provides a fully implemented trace-driven learning architecture that supports synthetic invariant discovery, synthetic skill construction, synthetic transfer, capability-oriented metrics, and end-to-end experimentation. The framework serves as a testbed for studying these mechanisms, but the strongest theoretical claims regarding transfer-aware superiority, SCT superiority, capability growth, intelligence growth, and ARC external validity remain unestablished.

14J-9 Publication Claim Ceiling

The maximum scientifically defensible claim is:

In controlled synthetic grid-task environments, ASFT demonstrates repeatable trace recurrence detection, skill construction, reuse, and transfer mechanisms. These mechanisms do not yet establish transfer-aware ranking superiority, superior skill-selection policies, robust capability-growth interpretation, intelligence-growth interpretation, or replication on ARC tasks.

Anything stronger than this would exceed the evidence.

14J Final Verdict
Scientific Conclusion

ASFT successfully demonstrates a functioning synthetic learning pipeline that can discover recurring trace structures, construct reusable skills, and produce transfer effects within controlled environments.

However, the evidence does not support the strongest theoretical claims originally proposed. Transfer-aware ranking superiority, SCT policy superiority, capability-growth interpretation, intelligence-growth interpretation, and ARC external validity remain unresolved research questions rather than validated conclusions.
