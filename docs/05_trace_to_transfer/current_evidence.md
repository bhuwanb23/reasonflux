# Current Evidence and Claim Ceiling

## Summary

The project has an implemented synthetic ASFT prototype and extensive audits, but no validated ARC-AGI-3 skill-formation result. Use the Phase 14J conclusions and 13H evidence package as the authoritative scientific ceiling.

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| v0.1 synthetic pipeline | Under a known four-family generator, traces, reflection/rotation candidates, skills, reuse and cost-proxy changes can be produced/measured. | Open-ended skill discovery, AGI-3 generalization, intelligence growth, or a causal full ASFT chain. |
| Phase 13B | Transfer-aware ranking tied frequency-only/no-transfer on the corrected primary endpoint. | Ranking superiority or a universal claim that ranking never matters. |
| Phase 13C | 44 eligible synthetic candidate rows were all selected; SCT, always-create and random-create tied on the main endpoint; random-create degenerated to always-create. | SCT discrimination or general skill-creation superiority. |
| Phase 13G | Bounded ARC-AGI-2 adapter exact-fit counts were 3/600 discovery, 5/200 transfer, 2/200 holdout; zero invariant candidates/skills/capabilities. | ARC-AGI-3 performance or impossibility of invariant/skill formation. Preserve budget/config mismatch and holdout-exposure caveats. |
| Phase 14 synthesis | The full ASFT chain and intelligence interpretation remain unestablished; transfer-to-capability is a major unresolved bridge. | Any broad intelligence-growth claim. |
| Local ARC-AGI-3 plan | A design draft specifies interaction/skill logging; no runs/results are recorded. | An AGI-3 claim, score, or confirmed implementation. |
