## Acceptance, unresolved fields, and boundary

P5 is complete as a **reviewable protocol proposal**, not a preregistration, implementation, or authorization. Alpha/confidence level, effect and regression margins, sample size/power, exact source/target manifests/cohort, task IDs/split, randomization/reset feasibility, numerical resource caps, prediction loss/abstention operating point, harm adjudication, missingness procedure, named owners/reviewers, and freeze dates remain `UNSET`. No threshold may be tuned after outcomes. The protocol cannot proceed to execution until all unresolved fields are prospectively settled and existing approvals/data-access gates are satisfied. No code was written; no data were accessed and no experiment or benchmark was run.
