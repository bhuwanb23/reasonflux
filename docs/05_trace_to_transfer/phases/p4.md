# P4 — Counterexample boundary algorithm

**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope, 2026-11-04. Completion does not authorize code, experiments, benchmarks, ARC runs, split assignment, candidate/skill execution, or restricted-outcome access.

## Objective

Specify candidate extraction, contradiction handling, boundary refinement, and runtime abstention/fallback without conflating errors with invariant evidence or using evaluation outcomes to validate versions they refined.

## Inputs and dependencies

- Existing [algorithm sketch](../counterexample_boundary_algorithm.md), reconciled to the versioned P4 contract.
- [P2 formal skill-object contract](../formal_skill_object.md) and [P2 schema](../p2/schema_v1.json).
- [P3 Claim N/fair replay controls](../p3/fair_replay_controls_v1.md).
- Dependencies and restrictions in the [T2T master plan](../master_plan.md).

## Work sequence and outcomes

1. **P4.1 — Candidate generation:** complete. Defined pinned, versioned normalization; deterministic structural trace alignment; template-based motif/candidate proposals; explicit unknown/ambiguity handling; and support/contradiction/exclusion ledgers. Candidates remain non-executable and no support threshold is invented.
2. **P4.2 — Counterexample taxonomy:** complete. Defined ordered prediction/permission/observation/action/effect/attribution triage, P2 enum interoperability, and separate missing-condition/uncertainty diagnostics. Unresolved cases stay unresolved.
3. **P4.3 — Scope refinement:** complete. Defined fail-closed quarantine, add/split/relax/reject proposals, immutable successor versioning, coverage accounting, and prospective evidence roles. A result used for refinement cannot validate the successor or its descendants.
4. **P4.4 — Fallback:** complete. Defined preflight, tri-state applicability, parameter binding, prediction freeze, step/resource checks, mismatch stop, authorized fallback/deopt, and append-only logging.

## Acceptance, unresolved decisions, and boundary

P4 is complete for documentation/design scope: deterministic conditional flow, taxonomy and P2 mapping, version/evidence rules, data-use boundary, and fallback semantics are stated. The flow halts or holds when its representation, identity, relation-template, taxonomy, promotion, or runtime policies are unset; it does not invent defaults. Exact state/action/effect ontology, relation-template inventory, variableization/alignment policy, support/refinement/rejection thresholds, adjudication rubric, data manifests, hash/ID profile, validation source, and action/step/time/compute caps remain open for later design and approvals. P4 creates no candidate/skill, does not inspect/classify outcomes, and implements or runs nothing.
