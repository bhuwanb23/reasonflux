## Acceptance, current ceiling and boundary

P7 is a **reporting and evaluation design**, not a result or run-ready preregistration. Claim A has limited known-generator synthetic support only; it does not transfer to ARC-AGI-3. N/B/C/D/E remain untested for the proposed AGI-3 method. P1 standalone novelty remains a no-go; comparative reframe is provisional and not novelty-cleared. No empirical claim may be written without a matching authorized run artifact and frozen claim criteria.

All numerical criteria and eligibility/assignment details left unset by P5 remain unset: alpha/confidence, minimum effects/regression margins, sample size/power, cohort/splits, resource caps, metric operating points, missingness method and named owners/reviewers. Code, notebook, official entry/team linkage, evaluator receipt, score, live eligibility and sharing permissions are unavailable or unresolved. P7 completion grants no engineering or data authorization.

The owner has said they may want to move to code after this design sequence. That is a future direction, not an implementation authorization: any coding scope must be requested/authorized separately, and cannot bypass remaining project/data/competition gates. No P7 implementation, tests, experiments, benchmarks, ARC runs, split assignments or outcome access occurred.
