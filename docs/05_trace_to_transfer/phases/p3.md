# P3 — Claim N and fair replay controls

**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope, 2026-11-04. Completion does not authorize code, experiments, benchmarks, ARC runs, split assignment, outcome-based tuning, or restricted-outcome access.

## Objective

Define a fair conceptual test of whether an explicit invariant representation adds value beyond raw and strong trace replay/parameterized compression, without conflating the representation effect with counterexample-based boundary learning.

## Inputs and dependencies

- [Claim N, version `T2T-ClaimN-v1.0.0-draft`](../claim_n_specification.md)
- [P2 formal skill-object contract](../formal_skill_object.md)
- Dependencies and existing restrictions in the [T2T master plan](../master_plan.md)

## Work sequence and outcomes

1. **P3.1 — Raw replay:** complete. Defined R0 as exact ordered replay with fixed source-context assumptions; mismatch stops/abstains rather than repairing, adapting, or rebinding.
2. **P3.2 — Strong replay:** complete. Defined R1's source-only compression recipe, retrieval, typed slot binding (including new IDs under fixed schema rules), aligned-effect prediction interface, bounded source-derived control flow, common wrapper, and competence review. R1 is the required primary comparator; raw replay alone is insufficient. Counterexample-derived invariant boundaries and target outcomes are excluded from construction.
3. **P3.3 — Isolation and budget matching:** complete. Specified common information/evaluation and PredictionRecord output, a resource-vector matching matrix, R0/R1/I0/I1 ablations, coverage/selective-risk and intention-to-treat behavioral estimands, interpretation rules, and falsifiers. Counterexample boundary updates remain a separate Claim B factor.

## Deliverable

Versioned contract [`T2T-P3-FairReplay-v1.0.0-draft`](../p3/fair_replay_controls_v1.md), summarized in the [P3 packet](../p3/README.md), with reconciled [Claim N specification](../claim_n_specification.md) and three completed subphase records.

## Acceptance, open choices, and boundary

P3 is complete for the documentation/design scope: baseline capabilities, allowed transformations, mismatch handling, matched information/resources, endpoints, ablations, and invalid/inconclusive outcomes are specified consistently. Exact baseline implementation/reviewer qualifications, data manifests, observation/slot correspondence, numerical resource caps, prediction loss and abstention operating point, behavior outcomes, cohort, sample size, randomization, uncertainty/multiplicity rules, and effect margins remain open for P5 and future owner/authorization gates. No baseline was built, no outcome was used for tuning, and no empirical claim or novelty clearance follows from P3.
