[`T2T-P7-ResultsShell-v1.0.0-draft`](../p7/results_shell_v1.md) and the required table/denominator rules in the [P7 plan](../p7/evaluation_reporting_v1.md#3-reporting-table-shell-and-denominator-rules-p72).

## Result

The shell covers run/data integrity; Claim A candidate formation; N; B; C/D; E; failures/deviations; cost vector; and reproducibility/paper linkage. Placeholders are explicit (`NOT_RUN`, `UNSET`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNKNOWN`, `PENDING_REVIEW`). It requires numerator, denominator, unit, role, missingness, reason and provenance. Failures, abstentions, timeouts, fallbacks, no-candidate cases, missing values, and resource breaches are not silently omitted or written as zero. Costs stay a vector; no composite score is introduced.

## Unresolved and boundary

Future run-specific denominators, metric operating points, uncertainty/multiplicity settings, exact resource units/caps and cohort identifiers remain unset. This is a blank reporting shell; it contains no outcomes or fabricated result values and does not authorize data access or a run.
