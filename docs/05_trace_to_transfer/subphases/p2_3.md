# P2.3 — Evidence and lifecycle

**Parent:** [T2T-P2](../phases/p2.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Append-only evidence/lifecycle contract in the [formal skill object specification](../formal_skill_object.md), machine-readable record shapes in the [P2 JSON Schema](../p2/schema_v1.json), and expected behavior in the [interoperability fixtures](../p2/interoperability_fixtures.md).

## Result

Pinned evidence snapshots reference permitted source events and carry explicit evidence roles. Observed, inferred, counterexample, counterfactual-activation, and evaluator-outcome records are distinct. A `PredictionRecord` with all required applicability predicates TRUE is frozen before the target event; a later `EffectAssessment` references it and a separate observed-effect record. Outcomes remain isolated and cannot inform earlier candidate/promotion decisions. `TRUE`, `FALSE`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` are not conflated; unknown applicability yields abstention.

Candidate and skill status are separate append-only lifecycles. Candidate decisions do not silently produce executable skills; structural eligibility is not validation; validation is not active use; active use requires separate authorization. Material contradiction, likely harm, stale dependency, or integrity concern can quarantine a specific immutable version. Revision creates a successor with a new digest; quarantine does not rewrite or reactivate the old version. Retirement preserves lineage.

## Thresholds and boundary

No support, recurrence, confidence, validation, promotion, or resource threshold is set. Future criteria must be justified and frozen prospectively, never lowered after observing yield. No hidden/test/confirmatory outcomes or split labels are authorized for evidence creation. P2.3 defines a design contract only; no candidate, lifecycle transition, or experiment was run.
