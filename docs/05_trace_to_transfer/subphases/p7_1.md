# P7.1 — Claim decisions

**Parent:** [T2T-P7](../phases/p7.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope, 2026-11-04; not result adjudication or authorization.

## Deliverable

Claim decision vocabulary and A/N/B/C/D/E support/weakening matrix in the [versioned P7 plan](../p7/evaluation_reporting_v1.md#2-decision-states-and-evidence-gate-p71).

## Result

Kept run validity (`NOT_RUN`, `VALID`, `DEVIATION_REVIEW`, `INVALID`) separate from claim decisions (`UNTESTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `WEAKENED`, `SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE`). Invalid runs do not become valid null results. `SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE` requires a valid authorized run and all prospectively frozen criteria; `WEAKENED` requires a relevant valid falsifier; `INCONCLUSIVE` is not a no-effect finding; `UNTESTED` means no eligible authorized evidence addresses the estimand.

Mapped each claim to the P5 primary outcome, contrast, support conditions and weakening patterns. Claim A's limited synthetic evidence is explicitly not AGI-3 support; N/B/C/D/E remain untested for the proposed AGI-3 method. No downstream claim is inferred from upstream results.

## Unresolved and boundary

Numerical criteria and operating points remain `UNSET` under P5: alpha/confidence, delta-minimum, coverage/abstention rules, sample size/power, harm/regression/cost guardrails and multiplicity family. No result state has been assigned from empirical data.
