# P4.1 — Candidate generation

**Parent:** [T2T-P4](../phases/p4.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Deterministic, source-only proposal pipeline in the [P4 boundary contract](../p4/boundary_algorithm_v1.md#3-p41--candidate-generation).

## Result

The procedure validates permission/version refs; preserves raw observations; normalizes through pinned state/action/effect schemas; keeps unknowns explicit; forms transition views only from linkable observations/actions/effects; and enumerates maximal common contiguous structural alignments in stable order. It retains all ambiguous alignments rather than selecting a favorable one. A frozen relation-template library instantiates only typed fields with explicit falsifiers; candidates remain non-executable `CandidateVersion` hypotheses; `PROPOSED` is represented by a separate genesis `LifecycleEvent`, not a mutable status field.

A separate proposal ledger records occurrences, unique event/trace/run/context counts, support/contradiction/qualification/control/unresolved roles, duplicates, exclusions, and unknowns. Counts are descriptive—not independent-trial claims or promotion criteria. No support or recurrence threshold is set; candidates are not automatically accepted or converted to skills.

## Unresolved and boundary

Exact ARC ontology/normalizer, relation-template inventory, variableization policy, motif-size/recurrence rules, approved identity/hash profile, and source manifests remain open. If required inputs are unpinned, the pipeline blocks rather than guessing. No source records were processed and no candidate was generated in P4.
