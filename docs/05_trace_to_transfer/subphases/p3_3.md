# P3.3 — Isolation and budget matching

**Parent:** [T2T-P3](../phases/p3.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Common comparison rules, budget-matching vector, ablations, estimands, interpretation table, and falsifiers in the [P3 fair replay contract](../p3/fair_replay_controls_v1.md#4-common-evaluation-and-information-controls-p33).

## Result

- Match the permitted source manifest, observation/action schema, model/checkpoint and tool access, context/history, common retrieval interface, target/evaluator conditions, and declared storage/context/compute/action opportunities. Report actual consumption and creation/validation/invocation costs separately; no weighted composite. If a confound or budget mismatch could explain the result, call the comparison inconclusive rather than matched.
- Required arms: R0 raw replay; R1 competent strong replay; I0 invariant semantics with counterexample updates held fixed. Each uses the same prediction/abstention interface, though R0/R1 may only transport aligned source effects. I1 with counterexample updates is a separate Claim B factor, not part of the primary Claim N estimate.
- Primary mechanism endpoint: prediction coverage and selective risk/error; compare risk only at a predeclared common coverage/operating point or through a loss that explicitly prices abstention. Conditional accuracy alone cannot reward abstaining on difficult cases. Secondary: intention-to-treat behavior across all assigned units, including abstentions/failures, with task score/progress and actions reported separately.
- Core falsifiers include R1 matching/beating I0, an equivalent relation-stripped ablation, gains explained by compression/parameterization alone, or gains disappearing under matched budgets. Outcome leakage, unreviewed baseline weakness, or material mismatches invalidate or make the comparison inconclusive.

## Unresolved and boundary

Exact budgets, score/loss and abstention rule, cohort/split, sample size, pairing/reset feasibility, evaluator, uncertainty/multiplicity method, effect margins, and permissions remain unset for P5 and existing authorization gates. No split, outcome tuning, experiment, benchmark, or ARC run was performed or authorized.
