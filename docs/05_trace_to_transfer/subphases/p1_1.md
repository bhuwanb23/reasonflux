# P1.1 — Source inventory

**Parent:** [T2T-P1](../phases/p1.md)  
**Status:** documentation/research-design only; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Purpose

Build a reproducible bibliography across trace-to-skill, program/library learning, options, counterexample-guided synthesis, safe transfer and ARC-AGI-3 agents.

## Required work

- State definitions and assumptions explicitly; cite source documents and versions.
- Document alternatives considered, unresolved decisions, and how each would affect the central claim.
- Keep source data, evidence provenance, and claim interpretation auditable.
- Ask for owner review before treating any consequential ambiguity as resolved.

## Guardrails

Capture version/date, primary source, supplement, benchmark, controls and claims.

## Deliverable and review test

Search log and citations; search-index snippets do not count as primary evidence.

## Current working artifact

**P1.1 delivered for the owner-approved idea-stage scope.** See [`P1 source inventory`](../p1/source_inventory.md). It distinguishes primary sources from search leads, adds a targeted search/citation-chase record, and records direct mechanism-level relevance. It is intentionally not exhaustive: full supplement/repository review and broad citation-graph traversal remain pre-submission novelty-clearance work.

The reviewer should be able to determine what was decided, what remains unknown, and what evidence would falsify or revise the design without executing an experiment. Any numerical threshold, split, or eligibility rule not already approved must remain unset.
