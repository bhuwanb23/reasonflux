# P2.2 — Invariant versus replay

**Parent:** [T2T-P2](../phases/p2.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Operational distinction in the [formal skill object contract](../formal_skill_object.md), supported by the [P2 interoperability fixtures](../p2/interoperability_fixtures.md).

## Result

Defined three comparators/treatments:

1. **Raw replay:** stored sequence under recorded assumptions.
2. **Strong parameterized/compressed replay:** best-effort trace-derived reusable procedure with typed slots and parameter rebinding; it must not be weakened.
3. **Invariant skill:** explicit versioned relation over typed normalized state/action/effect variables, with applicability predicates and prospective effect prediction.

An invariant-specific semantic delta requires an explicit falsifiable relation, observable pre-outcome conditions, evidence scope beyond exact source-event replay, a permitted held-out context that can distinguish the representation, and an ablation preserving wrapper/parameters/retrieval/budgets as far as possible. If the strong replay comparator matches, or no target context distinguishes the representation, Claim N is unsupported or unidentifiable. Compression, parameter binding, conditional invocation, or better engineering alone does not establish invariant value.

## Open choices and boundary

Exact ARC representation, relation DSL, minimum recurrence/context breadth, and resource-matching rules remain open for later design; no thresholds were invented. The P1 no-go on broad mechanism novelty remains in force. This is a research-design contract, not a claim that the proposed representation is novel or effective. No experiment, benchmark, ARC run, split, or hidden-outcome access is authorized.
