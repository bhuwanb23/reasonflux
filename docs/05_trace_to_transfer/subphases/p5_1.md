# P5.1 — Arms and estimands

**Parent:** [T2T-P5](../phases/p5.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not preregistration, implementation, or authorization.

## Deliverable

Claim/arm matrix in the [versioned P5 protocol](../p5/protocol_v1.md#2-p51--claim-specific-arms-and-estimands), aligned to the [P3 Claim N controls](../p3/fair_replay_controls_v1.md) and [claim-evidence map](../../../reports/competition/arc_prize_2026_claim_evidence_map_v1.md).

## Result

Separated candidate-generation arms (`Ginv` versus `Grec`) from runtime arms: `B0` no learned skills, `R0` raw replay, `R1` strong replay, `I0` invariant with boundary updates fixed, `I1` invariant plus counterexample boundary, and `I−rel` relation ablation. Claim-specific primary contrasts are: A, candidate generalization `Ginv` vs `Grec`; N, I0 vs R1; B, I1 vs I0 on boundary quality; C, I1 vs I0 on harmful transfer; D, I1 vs I0/common controls on action efficiency; E, full I1 against the no-skill, replay, and recurrence baselines. Arms/units and interpretation rules are specified; Claim outcomes are not inferred along the chain.

## Unresolved and boundary

Actual arm artifacts, cohort/assignment, score operating point, cost caps, MME, sample size, and multiplicity family remain unset. No arm was implemented or run; no split or task IDs were assigned.
