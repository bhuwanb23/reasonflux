# P4.3 — Scope refinement

**Parent:** [T2T-P4](../phases/p4.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Version-safe refinement rules in the [P4 boundary contract](../p4/boundary_algorithm_v1.md#5-p43--scope-refinement-versioning-and-evidence-separation), with edge cases in the [decision fixtures](../p4/decision_fixtures.md).

## Result

A material scope/dynamics contradiction stops the invocation and may quarantine its immutable SkillVersion. It never edits the version in place. A proposed specialization enumerates predicates from a frozen observable vocabulary; known FALSE-at-miss predicates yield separate draft successor candidates `G AND q`, with support/coverage ledgers. Unknown predicates cannot refine. No exact event/level/screenshot blacklist or hidden condition is invented. A candidate remains unpromoted; one verified event can support safety containment and a proposal, not validation or promotion.

Condition removal requires fresh permitted evidence and a prospective redundancy/generalization policy. Scope splits create separate successor proposals; unknown cases abstain. Reliable deterministic-relation falsification may reject a CandidateVersion; stochastic/noisy cases without a frozen criterion remain held/unresolved. Every event has prospective evidence roles: a target result used to refine a successor cannot validate that successor or its descendants; restricted/hidden/test/confirmatory outcomes are never refinement data.

## Unresolved and boundary

Support/refinement/rejection thresholds, predicate DSL, independent adjudication, fresh validation source, and allowed data manifests remain open. P4 creates no split, candidate population, updated boundary, or successor version and performs no validation.
