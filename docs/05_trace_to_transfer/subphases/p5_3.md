# P5.3 — Metrics

**Parent:** [T2T-P5](../phases/p5.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not preregistration, implementation, or authorization.

## Deliverable

Claim-linked endpoint and denominator specification in the [P5 protocol](../p5/protocol_v1.md#4-p53--outcomes-metrics-and-denominators).

## Result

- Official ARC-AGI-3 evaluator score/progress remains the task outcome; no in-house score replaces it.
- Claim N reports prediction coverage and selective risk/error; risk comparisons require a predeclared common operating point or loss that accounts for abstention.
- Claim B reports boundary coverage, false activation, false rejection when independently adjudicated, unknowns, and denominators separately.
- Claims C/D report harmful-transfer categories, intention-to-treat rates, action efficiency, and score separately. Invocation-conditional rates are descriptive; low use alone is not safety.
- Creation, validation, storage, retrieval, inference, wall-time, compute, action and fallback costs are a vector. No unvalidated weighted composite is introduced.
- All eligible, assigned, attempted, failed, abstained, timed-out, fallback and missing-evaluator units remain in the flow/denominator ledger with reasons.

## Unresolved and boundary

Exact prediction loss/abstention cost, official cohort aggregation, harm adjudication, score guardrails and resource units/caps remain unset. No metric was computed and no task/evaluator result was accessed.
