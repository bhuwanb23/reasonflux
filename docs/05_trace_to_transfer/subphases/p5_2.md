**Parent:** [T2T-P5](../phases/p5.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not preregistration, implementation, or authorization.

## Deliverable

Prospective data-role, cohort-eligibility, reset, and resource-vector proposal in the [P5 protocol](../p5/protocol_v1.md#3-p52--permitted-data-cohort-and-budgets), consistent with P3/P4 evidence rules.

## Result

Defined source/formation, development/refinement, validation, confirmatory/target, and restricted/hidden/test roles. Specified a track-specific manifest field list, target eligibility criteria without task IDs, and prospective role/version assignment. A validation result may assess only its frozen version; if reused for successor refinement, it cannot validate that successor or descendants. No source/target artifacts, cohort size, task IDs, split labels, or actual split assignment are included.

The matching vector covers information/source exposure, model/tools, observation/context, memory/storage, retrieval, construction/validation, invocation compute/time, actions, and evaluation. Pair/reset analysis is allowed only where environment rules permit; otherwise use an authorized episode/game unit. Exact caps remain unset.

## Unresolved and boundary

Permitted manifest/cohort, task-family separation, model/evaluator, randomization, reset/fork feasibility, numerical caps, and access approvals remain open. No data were opened or assigned and no run was authorized.
