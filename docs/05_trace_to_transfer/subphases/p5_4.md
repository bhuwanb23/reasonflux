# P5.4 — Analysis and falsifiers

**Parent:** [T2T-P5](../phases/p5.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not preregistration, implementation, or authorization.

## Deliverable

Estimation/uncertainty proposal, multiplicity, exclusions, effect-threshold fields, stop rules and claim-specific falsifiers in the [P5 protocol](../p5/protocol_v1.md#5-p54--analysis-uncertainty-exclusions-and-falsifiers).

## Result

Specified game/episode-clustered uncertainty, paired contrasts only when pairing is legitimate, one primary estimand per claim, and a proposed Holm family-wise procedure over all prospectively designated primary confirmatory contrasts. Exclusions are frozen before assignment; post-assignment failures, timeouts, abstentions, and fallbacks stay in ITT denominators. Data/access/version/resource violations trigger a hold/stop, not a silent exclusion. No efficacy/futility peeking or early effect stop is proposed.

Claim-specific falsifiers cover candidate non-generalization (A), R1 matching I0 or relation ablation equivalence (N), no boundary gain or coverage collapse (B), no harm reduction (C), no action-efficiency gain or unacceptable regression (D), and failure against required baselines/cost rules (E).

## Unresolved and boundary

Alpha/confidence level, MME and regression margins, sample size/power, exact missingness method, family composition, cost/harm thresholds, named roles and freeze dates remain `UNSET`. The protocol is not run-ready until these are prospectively resolved and separately authorized. No implementation, analysis, experiment, split or data access occurred.
