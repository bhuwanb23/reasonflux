# P4.2 — Counterexample taxonomy

**Parent:** [T2T-P4](../phases/p4.md)  
**Status:** COMPLETE for documentation/design scope; not a readiness gate or authorization.

## Deliverable

Ordered triage and P2 interoperability mapping in the [P4 boundary contract](../p4/boundary_algorithm_v1.md#4-p42--counterexample-taxonomy-and-triage), with synthetic cases in the [decision fixtures](../p4/decision_fixtures.md).

## Result

Triage requires a matching immutable pre-outcome PredictionRecord. The ordered checks are: permissions/version integrity; observation reliability; action-execution fidelity; local-vs-evaluator outcome; effect comparison; then attribution. P2 top-level classes remain `DYNAMICS_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, `SCOPE_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, `EXECUTION_ERROR`, `OBSERVATION_ERROR`, `OUTCOME_ONLY`, and `AMBIGUOUS_UNRESOLVED`.

P4 diagnostic subtypes separately mark observation uncertainty and a missing-condition hypothesis in a versioned sidecar. A missing condition is not a proven cause: it must use a pinned, observable predicate and remain unresolved until the applicable evidence policy supports it. Execution/perception faults do not narrow the invariant; task-level failure alone is not a local counterexample; ambiguity stays unknown.

## Unresolved and boundary

The domain adjudication rubric, human/reviewer roles, and statistical criteria for noisy/stochastic effects remain open. If prediction ordering, observation fidelity, action fidelity, or attribution cannot be established, the result is unresolved and cannot become guard evidence. No outcome or counterexample was inspected or classified in P4.
