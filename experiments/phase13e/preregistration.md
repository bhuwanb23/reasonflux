# Phase 13E preregistration — PHASE-13E-CAP-GROWTH-V1

**Status:** protocol and machine-readable configuration are frozen before any Phase 13E confirmation outcomes.  
**Independent seed block:** 60–69 (ten seeds, disjoint from the Phase 13D block 50–59).  
**Evidence class:** synthetic grid benchmark only; this is not ARC or external validation.

## Repository preflight and scope

The repository already contains capability formation, validation, lifecycle, coverage, utility, portfolio, growth, composition, and intelligence APIs. This experiment will call those APIs and will not modify global metric definitions. Skill candidates come from the existing trace and invariant pipelines; capabilities are proposed with `propose_capabilities_from_library(..., require_valid=True)` and are not inserted manually. Prior Phase 13C/13D limitations remain in scope: count/group skills and higher-order composition may be unavailable, and missing outcomes will remain null with explicit reasons.

Two intelligence formulas exist. The confirmatory primary is frozen as `evaluation.growth_metrics.compute_ig`, using the existing `GrowthConfig` weights (CGR .35, TG .25, UG .20, CCG .15, CompG .05); IGR and IE use `evaluation.intelligence.compute_igr` and `compute_ie`. This choice aligns the Phase 13E CGM hypothesis with the configured CGR/transfer/utility/coverage/composition components. `intelligence_score` / `compute_system_intelligence_state` is not an alternate confirmatory pathway and will not replace it after outcomes. The formula, inputs, aggregation, and weights are specified in `config.json`.

The roadmap's expected `C_t` example above 1 is illustrative. Phase 13E uses the repository's bounded CapU-aligned score and will not rescale it to match that example. C_t is the mean score of non-archived, validated/proposed portfolio members; an empty portfolio is 0.0. CGR is the difference between adjacent round C_t values divided by the fixed one-round interval. Coverage uses the repository capability-coverage API, reported both as portfolio coverage and as the union of successful transfer target families divided by four. The IG CCG input is the round-to-round delta of that global supported-family fraction (not the sum of per-capability coverages). For Phase 13E-created skills, measured TS is the mean observed positive benefit among successful ReuseEngine transfers; expected transfer priors are kept separate and are not used as measured TS. System-level SU and TS averages use all non-provisional skills in that arm; portfolio CapU is restricted to validated capability members. Task utility is observed success minus reasoning cost/100, falling back to the baseline solver when reuse does not apply. Experience count is the 32 discovery traces plus cumulative held-out tasks through that round, identically denominated across arms. No thresholds, formulas, arms, or criteria will be adjusted after seeing confirmation outcomes.

## Design

- Discovery: 8 generated traces per family, difficulty 0.3, seed `run_seed + 13000`. The existing trace and invariant pipelines are used. The common candidate gate is the Phase 13C shared non-SCV qualification gate. Primary SCT selection is the frozen Phase 13C SCV10 rule `SCV10 > 0`; SCV itself is not retuned.
- Evaluation: four sequential rounds, four held-out tasks per family per round, difficulty 0.3, with the same generated tasks across arms within each seed. Round 2 includes the task generator's symmetric count/group variants to permit genuine reflection-invariance transfer. Rounds 3–4 include the generator's actual rotate-then-reflect composed tasks for reflection. Round 3 is the existing composition candidate's training/validation and promotion opportunity; round 4 uses independent task seeds as held-out composition confirmation. The composition-value criterion uses only round-4 incremental benefit over the best single component. Outcomes are scored by the repository GridSolver and ReuseEngine (with the promoted ordered chain executed as a chain on composed tasks); task- and seed-level rows are retained.
- Capabilities: proposals are generated from active/growing/core skill library members with the existing proposal and validation APIs. Validity requires the configured repository transfer/evidence constraints. Lifecycle and utility are advanced using existing APIs. A proposal is not counted as a capability until it passes validation.
- Composition: propose sequential chains from learned skills, evaluate on matching held-out composed tasks, record real success/benefit, and promote only after the existing composition lifecycle permits it. No component skill is hand-created. If a needed learned component or proposal is absent, that composition outcome is unavailable.
- Arms: ASFT; B1 no learning; B2 skill-storage-only (stores/reuses learned skills and permits only atomic capabilities after the existing skill lifecycle marks a skill core/anchored; no clustering or composition); B3 random creation from the common eligible pool; B4 a transparent compression-only proxy based on repeated observed programs (not canonical DreamCoder); A1 no IDT (family-specific exact full trace sequences repeated at least three times; no invariant representation); A2 no SCT (all commonly qualified candidates); A3 no persistent skill library; A4 no composition; A5 no CGM (same behavior as ASFT with growth outputs deliberately masked/unavailable). A5 is a measurement ablation, not an outcome-performance comparator.
- Adversarial robustness: add non-executable dummy skills only in an isolated EXP-CAP-22 measurement fixture; they have no transfer events, are not learned, and cannot enter the validated capability set. Also report fragmentation, natural saturation, and an isolated EXP-CAP-21 probe that removes the lexicographically first measured successful-transfer skill from all memberships in a copied final ASFT portfolio before recomputing C_t and CGR.

## Frozen outcomes and criteria

The exact machine-readable criteria are in `config.json`. The ten seed-level observations are the independent units. Report mean, sample SD, and two-sided 95% Student-t CI using `df=n−1` for the available independent seed-level values (for complete n=10 metrics, df=9 and critical value `2.262157`) for CGR, IG, IGR, coverage, and utility. Paired arm contrasts use within-seed differences on identical task streams. Correlations (TS↔CGR, SU↔CGR, coverage↔IG, composition↔IG) are Pearson correlations of each seed's arithmetic mean across four rounds (n=10), labeled descriptive. EXP-CAP-10/11 additionally report per-seed mean accepted-capability CapU versus mean member measured TS and mean member reuse count, also as descriptive Pearson correlations across seeds. All six relationships are reported with n, r, and a two-sided Fisher-z 95% CI; positive r is directionally supportive only, with no significance threshold or causal claim. Future prediction pairs C_t at rounds 1–3 with mean task utility in rounds 2–4; a 4,000-replicate seed-clustered bootstrap with fixed RNG seed 87213 supplies the interval, and the result is unavailable if the data are insufficient or constant.

Primary acceptance decisions: at least one valid capability must persist across two consecutive rounds in at least 8/10 ASFT seeds; positive directional outcomes require a positive mean and 95% CI lower bound above zero; IE trend is the OLS slope across rounds 1–4 and must also have a positive mean and 95% CI lower bound above zero; ASFT-vs-baseline requires paired CI lower bounds above zero for both final C_t and mean held-out task utility across all four rounds, separately for each mandatory baseline; A1–A4 reduce CGR only if paired ASFT-minus-ablation CI lower bound is above zero; composition creates value only if its paired benefit over the best single component has a positive mean and CI lower bound above zero; A5 is necessarily unavailable. EXP-CAP-12 reports the fraction of seeds with a higher capability count in round 4 than round 2 alongside a successful held-out composition; EXP-CAP-14 reports accepted emergent-type seed-capability occurrences and remains unavailable if none exist. These are descriptive, with no manual insertion or post-hoc threshold. Failed, null, contradictory, and unavailable outcomes remain visible in the evidence package.

## Evidence package

The run will write frozen run metadata/source hashes, all seed-round-arm and task-level rows, six figures, six CSV tables, and a paper-ready report under `reports/experiments/PHASE-13E-CAP-GROWTH-V1/`. Confirmation starts only after contract tests pass and source/config hashes are recorded. Analysis may read saved records only; it may not rerun outcomes or change the frozen runner.
