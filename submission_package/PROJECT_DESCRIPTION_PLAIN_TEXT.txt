TITLE
Evaluating Claims, Not Just Systems

SUBTITLE
A longitudinal evidence audit of one ARC research program

PROJECT DESCRIPTION (306 words)

This project is a longitudinal claim-audit case study of one ARC-motivated research cycle. Rather than presenting a new solver or benchmark score, it traces what the project’s archived evidence supports at each step: mechanism occurrence, task-relevant utility, comparator-based superiority, generalization, and final scientific disposition.

The audit follows two evidence paths. ASFT produced recurring operation-level patterns and reflection/rotation skill objects in controlled synthetic tasks, with some observed reuse and simulator-step savings. Those observations support narrow mechanism occurrence and local utility; they do not establish incremental superiority. Transfer-aware ranking tied simpler controls, skill-creation policies did not clear their primary superiority gates, and capability metrics were mixed across phases. A bounded ARC-AGI-2 adapter had very low fit coverage and produced no accepted downstream objects, so ARC transfer is not demonstrated.

BL1 proposed counterexample-guided applicability-boundary learning. A bounded prior-art review found substantial overlap and stopped the line at P0, before implementation or experiment. This is a novelty/program decision, not an experimental efficacy result.

The result is not a single success/failure verdict. Pattern discovery receives partial support; skill formation and reuse are mixed; capability growth remains an unvalidated construct; ARC transfer is not demonstrated; and BL1 was terminated at P0. These dispositions have different evidence ceilings and should not be conflated.

Scope is intentionally narrow: this is a retrospective account of one research cycle, based on immutable saved records and a purposive literature screen, not a systematic review or field-wide prevalence study. T2T/S3 is excluded because its execution timestamp cannot be reconciled with the manuscript date. The archive remains unchanged; no S3 result or disposition is used here. No new experiment, benchmark run, statistical re-analysis, or post-hoc rescue is claimed.

The attached paper and visual assets make the evidence path inspectable: a claim ladder, a disposition map, a provenance boundary graphic, and the full longitudinal claim-stage matrix.
