# brag plan — ASFT (ReasonFlux)

## What it is
A research prototype for the ARC Prize Paper Track: **Adaptive Skill Formation Theory (ASFT)** — a theory and working scaffold arguing that intelligence grows by converting repeated reasoning traces into reusable skills that lower future reasoning cost.

## Who it's for / what it does for them
ARC Prize Paper Track reviewers and collaborators: gives them a clean, paper-ready thesis, metric definitions, experiment matrix, and a real Python repo scaffold (configs, src modules, baselines, reports, papers) instead of a notebook claim.

## What sets it apart
Not another ARC solver. Three named contributions — Invariant Discovery Theory, Skill Creation Trigger Theory, Capability Growth Metrics — wired end-to-end in code, with a data flow that reads like the thesis: Task → Trace → Invariant → Skill → Transfer → Growth → Reports. v0.1 is explicit about what it excludes (no LLMs, no neural nets, no meta-reasoner, no ARC-3 agents).

## Visual hook
The real pipeline as a song of modules: one clean motion graphic where each stage appears on a beat and hands off to the next, ending on the growth curve / paper framing.

## Tone
`polished` — serious, elegant, restrained. This is research, not a startup launch.

## One-line caption
Intelligence as growth, not just answers: ASFT turns repeated reasoning into reusable skills.
